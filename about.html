About L.U.M.A.

A little more about L.U.M.A.

A gentler way to notice how music affects your mood.

Your daily dose of kindness

☾

What L.U.M.A. does

L.U.M.A. looks at the music you listen to and helps you notice patterns in your mood. It can gently flag when your listening has been feeling unusually sad or low-energy for a while.

♫

How it works

Connect your music, and L.U.M.A. observes the mood and energy of what you listen to over time, so you can see gentle patterns in how it may be affecting you.

♥

Built for awareness

L.U.M.A. is not a music player. It is here to give you mood awareness and a space for gentle reflection, so you can check in with yourself without judgment.
